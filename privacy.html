Privacy Policy

Effective September 17, 2026. This policy explains what MotoRoute collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

The short version

  • You need an account (email, username, password) to use the app.
  • Your location is only shared while you turn Going Out on, and only with your mutual Close Friends and your current ride group.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third party analytics or tracking.
  • You can delete your account, and everything tied to it, from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, username, optional display name, and password. The password is stored only as a one way hash that we cannot read. Your email is used to verify your account and send password reset codes, and is never shown to other users.
  • Profile: an optional bio, the bikes you add, and an optional profile picture. The picture is cropped and shrunk on your phone before upload. The camera is used only when you choose Take Photo.
  • Location: used on your phone to centre the map, place hazard reports, build routes and give directions. When Going Out is on, your position and speed are sent to our server (about once a minute, or every few seconds while you lead a group), including while the app is in the background. We keep only your most recent position, not a history. The fastest speed recorded while Going Out is on is saved as your top speed.
  • Routes and activity: routes you create, ratings, comments, favorites, hazard reports and votes, and reports you file about comments or profile pictures.
  • Friends and groups: friend and Close Friend requests, group invitations and memberships, and the people you have muted or blocked.
  • Device token: if you allow notifications, the token Apple issues so we can send friend request and group invite alerts.
  • Technical logs: our server records the IP address and timing of requests to limit abuse, such as repeated sign in attempts.
  • Support and bug reports: if you email us or send a bug report from Settings, we receive your message, username, email address, app version, iOS version and iPhone model.

We do not collect advertising identifiers, contacts, health data or payment information. The app has no purchases.

How we use it

Only to run the app: showing your position, giving directions, sharing your location with the people you chose, showing routes and hazards to other riders, keeping the service safe, and answering support requests.

Who can see your information

  • Your email address is visible only to you.
  • Your username appears on routes and comments you post and to riders you interact with.
  • Your bio, bikes and profile picture are visible to your friends.
  • Your live position is visible to mutual Close Friends and to members of your current ride group, only while Going Out is on. Close Friends also see your top speed.
  • Public routes, their ratings and comments, and hazard reports are visible to signed in riders. Hazard reports never show who reported them.
  • Friends Only routes are visible to your friends. Private routes are visible only to you, except that if you lead a group ride on one, the members of that group receive it for that ride.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Service providers

  • Render hosts our server.
  • MongoDB Atlas stores account and content data.
  • Google (Gmail) delivers verification and password reset emails.
  • Apple provides maps, directions, weather forecasts for rain alerts, and push notifications. Map, directions and weather requests go from your phone straight to Apple and do not pass through our server.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Hazard reports expire on their own, from 2 hours to a maximum of 30 days depending on the type. Verification and password reset codes expire after 15 minutes. Sign in sessions last 7 days and all end when you change your password.

Reports and moderation

When you report a comment or a profile picture, the report is sent to our moderation inbox and reviewed by a person. Reports are anonymous to the person reported. You can mute or block any rider; blocking removes the friendship and stops all contact in both directions.

Deleting your account

Open Settings, then Account, then Delete Account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, profile, profile picture, routes, hazard reports, comments, ratings, votes, friendships and group memberships. You can also email MotoRouteTeam@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.

You can stop location sharing at any time by turning Going Out off, or by changing location access in iOS Settings.

Security

Connections to our server use HTTPS, passwords are hashed, and sign in attempts are rate limited. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify affected users if we become aware of a breach involving their information.

Children

MotoRoute is intended for licensed riders and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and note significant changes in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to MotoRouteTeam@gmail.com.
